How the mine works

The laws of the course sit on the surface. Every result is a crystal below, joined by tunnels to the results it uses. The deeper a crystal, the more it is built on.

Pick a crystal to see it. A pulsing crystal is ready to mine: walk its derivation one step at a time, choosing each next line. Finish and it turns gold, its tunnels light up, and the crystals below it open.

Mouse: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide. Touch: one finger turns, two pinch and slide.

Quest 3: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and press Enter VR. Trigger or pinch picks a crystal or a button on the panel. Grip grabs the mine, the thumbstick turns and zooms it, B or Y brings it back in front of you.
