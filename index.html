    A map of mathematics as a mine

    The axioms sit on the surface. Every theorem lies below everything its proof uses, and the tunnels follow the proofs. The deeper a crystal, the longer the chain of reasoning under it.

    Click a crystal to see what it says. Its whole ancestry lights up, all the way back to the axioms.

    Try:

    Mouse: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide. Touch: one finger turns, two pinch and slide. Keys: / search, U step up the critical path, H whole mine, Esc clear.
