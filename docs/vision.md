# Truth Mines — Vision

Oct 10, 2026 · @Dalton Gadapee

A place to explore mathematics where the surroundings are as beautiful as the ideas, the path is shaped by your own understanding, and you can always see a little of what lies further on.

## The feeling

The experience is designed to produce one feeling: your framework shifting. It is the sense that what you are moving through is changing how you think, that your understanding is advancing, and that new ideas are joining and reorganizing what you already know.

Two things feed it. The surroundings are as beautiful and inspiring as the mathematics they hold, so being there is a pleasure in itself. And you can always see a little further than you can reach, so you want to keep going to find out what lies beyond.

## Principles

Every brief and every coding session is checked against these five.

1. **The visuals come from the mathematics.** Each place looks and behaves the way its formalism thinks. Nothing is a generic room with a statement attached.
2. **Your path is your own.** There is no fixed curriculum. What you can reach next is decided by what you have already understood.
3. **Understanding is earned, not visited.** An idea joins your map because you did something that shows you grasp it, like predicting what the sieve leaves standing. Walking past it is not enough.
4. **You can always see further.** The edge of your map shows what you are now ready for, and fainter, what lies beyond that.
5. **The map is true.** The connections come from formal mathematics (set.mm), and every number shown is computed and tested. Beauty never bends the truth.

## The loop

The whole experience is one repeating loop, and its highlighted step is the feeling the project exists for.

(Diagram: see your frontier → enter a plane → earn the idea → your map rearranges → back to a new frontier.)

Each earned idea moves the frontier outward, so the loop never runs out of somewhere further to go.

## What this means for the existing work

Nothing built so far is wasted; each piece gets a clearer job.

| Piece | Its role under this vision |
| --- | --- |
| set.mm pipeline and dependency graph | Decides your frontier and how the map rearranges when you integrate an idea |
| Overview map | Becomes your personal map: lit where you understand, fogged elsewhere, glowing at the frontier |
| Planes (number theory first) | The places where you earn ideas through the click moments |
| Inside view (chambers and tunnels) | Under review: it shows structure but earns nothing; it may become how you travel between planes |
| Unreal spike | Paused; revisited once a plane is worth making beautiful |

The main new capability this needs is persistent progress: the app must remember what you have understood across visits.
